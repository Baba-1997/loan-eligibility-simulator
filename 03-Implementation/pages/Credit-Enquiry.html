Understand the credit enquiry

A formal loan application may involve a credit enquiry, which can affect your credit profile. This step helps you understand the process before continuing with your assessment.

✓

What this means for you

  • The credit enquiry may appear on your credit profile.
  • It helps verify your information and assess your creditworthiness.
  • A credit enquiry may affect your credit score, but it does not automatically mean your application will be declined.
