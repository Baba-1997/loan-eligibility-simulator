Understanding your credit profile

Your credit report contains information about your borrowing history, repayment behaviour and existing credit accounts.

Your credit score is a numerical indicator calculated using information from your credit profile. Different credit bureaus may calculate scores differently.

  • Check your credit report for incorrect information.
  • Pay your accounts on time.
  • Avoid taking on more debt than you can manage.
  • Monitor your credit profile regularly.

Understanding loan affordability

Affordability considers your income, living expenses, existing debt repayments and the estimated repayment on a new loan.

  • Review your monthly income and essential expenses.
  • Calculate how much you already spend on debt repayments.
  • Consider reducing non-essential expenses.
  • Allow room in your budget for unexpected costs.

Managing existing debt

Existing debt commitments include monthly repayments on loans, credit cards and other credit facilities.

  • List all your outstanding debt and monthly repayments.
  • Prioritise making required payments on time.
  • Consider paying down existing debt before borrowing more.
  • Contact your credit provider if you're struggling with repayments.

Understanding your estimated repayments

Your estimated monthly repayment depends on the loan amount, interest rate and repayment term.

  • A smaller loan amount generally reduces monthly repayments.
  • A longer repayment term may reduce monthly repayments but increase total interest.
  • Compare the total repayment amount, not only the monthly instalment.
  • Review your financial details before changing your requested amount.

The simulator uses an illustrative interest rate. Actual loan terms may differ.

Preparing for future borrowing

You don't need to apply for a loan immediately. You can take time to review your financial situation.

  • Create a realistic monthly budget.
  • Build an emergency savings fund where possible.
  • Reduce unnecessary expenses and existing debt.
  • Review your credit report periodically.
  • Reassess your affordability when your circumstances change.

Why we provide this information

These resources are designed to help you make informed decisions about borrowing. They do not change your estimated eligibility result, and they are not a loan offer or approval.

Tips for managing your finances

  • Keep track of your monthly income and expenses.
  • Try to pay your bills on time.
  • Reduce unnecessary debt where possible.
  • Review your budget regularly to stay on track.
